What Bamzal is

Bamzal is an AI growth consultant embedded in your Shopify admin. It uses the catalog, sales, cost and connected-channel data available for your store to identify opportunities and propose changes. These can include product-page drafts, pricing recommendations, retention plans, catalog improvements and campaign preparation for eligible accounts on Google, Meta, TikTok, Microsoft Advertising and Pinterest.

Start with analysis and recommendations. Supported changes require an enabled action, an eligible connected account, the necessary permissions and your approval of the applicable limits. Advanced automation and learning capabilities are conditional; check what is available for your store in the app before enabling them.

What the system covers

Five growth levers. Supported actions and advanced previews depend on your account and rollout.

Product Pages & Store Design

Drafts product titles, descriptions and page copy for review. Catalog analysis can highlight merchandising opportunities; improved conversion must be measured after a supported change is approved and applied.

Pricing

Uses available competitor and cost data to propose price changes with estimated impact. Check data freshness, your margin assumptions and the proposed limits before approval; recovery controls apply to supported price changes.

Customer Retention & Win-Back

Uses available customer and purchase history to propose re-engagement audiences and message drafts. Sending depends on channel eligibility, consent and approval. Measuring repeat purchases requires follow-up data; a suggested flow does not prove incremental revenue.

Catalog & Merchandising

Highlights catalog and merchandising opportunities using the product and performance data available. Review recommendations before applying supported storefront or feed changes; expected sales and margin effects remain estimates.

Paid Ads — when they're the right lever

Campaign preparation can cover Google Search, Shopping, Performance Max, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest and Microsoft Advertising where the action and account are eligible. Supported campaign creation uses paused mode for review. Account connection and a recommendation do not guarantee launch availability or profitable results.

Presence

Drafting and scheduling capabilities cover Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook, Threads and YouTube. Publishing requires the relevant connection, platform permissions and an enabled action. Organic availability is separate from paid campaign availability.

Creative

An in-app studio for copy, image and short-form video. Available generation tools depend on provider access, credits and feature eligibility. Creative DNA capabilities can propose variants; learning which attributes perform well requires attributable campaign outcomes.

Community

Draft and review replies in your brand voice for supported connected channels. Automated sending depends on the channel, permissions, configured limits and action enablement; connecting an inbox does not promise coverage of every comment or message.

Intelligence

Use available competitor observations and performance data to inform decisions. Profit forecasts are estimates with stated data requirements. Causal analysis and cross-store benchmarking are preview capabilities that require further outcome and privacy validation.

Optimization

Where enabled, scheduled reviews can assess bids, budgets, keyword waste, creative fatigue and inventory. Cadence and execution depend on the connected platform and rollout. Advisory and shadow modes do not authorize changes to ad delivery.

Governance

Supported paid actions pass through policy checks for credentials, budgets, rate limits and risk. Review recorded decisions and action-specific approval and recovery controls. The existence of a guard does not mean an action is enabled.

Learning & Evaluation — Preview

Preview capabilities can compare predictions with observed outcomes and propose changes to decision policies. They require sufficient, recent, comparable data and validation before broader use. A correlation score or evaluation window alone does not establish profitable results or authorize an action.

Compliance

Shopify GDPR webhooks (customer redact, shop redact, data request) wired. Meta GDPR data-deletion callback. EU AI Act Article 11 transparency surfaces. CSP nonce-based, OAuth 2.0 with PKCE where supported. TLS 1.2+ everywhere.

Paid-Ads Lever — Supported Channels

Campaign capabilities cover the networks below, with availability determined by the connected account, platform permissions and enabled actions. Store and cost analysis can inform your decision; it cannot establish that a campaign will be profitable.

Google Ads

Search, Shopping and Performance Max. Keyword discovery, GAQL reporting, ConversionActionService for ROAS tracking.

Meta (Facebook & Instagram)

Advantage+ Shopping, retargeting and prospecting via the Meta Marketing API. Creative variations per placement; Pages + Instagram Business for organic-native assets.

TikTok Ads

Video, Spark Ads and catalog campaigns via the TikTok Marketing API. AI-generated hook + CTA variations tuned to TikTok's native feel.

Pinterest Ads

Shopping campaigns, standard and Idea Pins via the Pinterest Ads API. Product feed synced directly from Shopify, visual-first creative briefs.

X Ads

Paid campaign launch on X is not currently offered in Bamzal. Check the app for changes in availability; organic posting has a separate connection and eligibility requirements.

Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) — managed on behalf of clients

Bamzal operates as a Tech Provider for Meta, creating and managing Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns on behalf of the Shopify merchants (business clients) who install our app. Merchants authorize access through Facebook Login for Business, granting our platform scoped permissions (ads_management, business_management, pages_show_list, instagram_basic) to publish campaigns into their own Meta Business & ad accounts. Merchants retain full ownership of their Meta assets and can revoke access at any time from Meta Business Integrations.
