Pricing FAQ

What's metered, and what isn't?

Monthly AI credits are shared across eligible AI features: Starter includes 5, Pro 200, Agency 1,000, and Enterprise 5,000. Free includes no monthly AI credits. Credit use varies by action and model; check the credit cost in the app before confirming. Credits are not a fixed number of images or videos. Additional credit packs are purchased separately through Shopify.

Who has access to my ad accounts?

You control your ad accounts. When you connect an eligible ad platform, Bamzal uses each platform's official OAuth 2.0 flow with the minimum scopes required to create and manage campaigns on your behalf. All campaigns are created in your own connected ad accounts. You can revoke access from inside the app at any time. Ad platforms are one of five levers — you only need to connect them if and when you want Bamzal to run paid ads.

What if I want manual control?

Start with recommendations and review supported changes before approval. Shadow mode evaluates without acting; advisory mode presents proposals. Execution within configured limits is available only for eligible, enabled actions. Connecting an account or choosing a plan does not by itself enable autonomous operation. Review the controls available for each action and pause future activity when needed.

Is there a free trial?

Your first paid subscription is eligible for a 7-day free trial. A previous paid subscription does not receive another trial when you change plans or reinstall. Shopify shows the trial, next billing date, and charge before you approve the subscription.

Do I pay separately for ad spend?

Yes. Bamzal subscription covers the software platform. Your ad budgets on Google Ads, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), TikTok and Pinterest are billed separately by each network directly to your own ad accounts on those platforms. Any campaign action still requires the permissions and limits configured for your store.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from within the Shopify admin. Canceling a subscription does not itself stop campaigns already running in your connected ad accounts. Pause them and confirm their status with the provider before disconnecting.
